Privacy Policy for Tyros

Effective Date: October 8, 2026 • Application: Tyros (com.tyreapp.tyre_app)

Tyros ("we", "our", or "us") provides a dealership management and customer follow-up application designed for tyre retailers, auto workshops, and service centers. We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users (including dealership staff, managers, and administrators) and their customer data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Tyros collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use the Tyros mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide shop operations management, daily sales reporting, and follow-up tracking, we collect the following types of information:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Login Credentials: Username, password hash, role (Administrator or Employee), and assigned dealership/branch shop ID.
  • Session & Auth Tokens: Secure authentication tokens stored encrypted on your device.

B. Customer Enquiry & Follow-up Data

  • Customer Details: Name, phone number, vehicle model, tyre brand, requested size, and service requirements entered by authorized staff.
  • Enquiry Status & Notes: Follow-up schedules, remarks, call outcomes, quotation notes, and completion statuses.

C. Daily Operations & Performance Data

  • Sales & Service Metrics: Counts of tyres sold (4-wheeler, 2-wheeler, 17"+ rims, commercial tyres) and services performed (Wheel Alignment, 2-Wheeler Balancing, Water Wash, Tyre Patches).
  • Shop Targets: Monthly sales targets and achievement summaries.

D. Diagnostic & Technical Information

  • Crash Logs & Performance Data: When an unexpected error occurs, diagnostic information may be captured via Sentry to help us improve app stability. Personal identifying information (PII) is automatically filtered and scrubbed prior to transmission.

2. How We Use the Information

Data Category Purpose of Processing
Customer Contact Info To allow dealership staff to track enquiry lifecycles and initiate follow-up calls or messages.
Follow-up Reminders To trigger scheduled local notifications on the staff device when a customer enquiry is due for follow-up.
Sales & Service Logs To calculate daily shop collections, staff productivity, and monthly sales target achievements.
Authentication Data To verify authorized access and ensure staff members only view data belonging to their assigned shop.
Diagnostics To identify application crashes, fix bugs, and optimize app performance.

3. Device Permissions Requested

The Tyros mobile application requests specific device permissions strictly necessary for its core operational functions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver timely reminders for due customer follow-ups and daily morning summaries.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to schedule precise alerts for specific follow-up appointments set by dealership staff.
  • Boot Receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to re-register pending notification reminders if your device is restarted.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to synchronize shop reports, targets, and enquiry updates with the secure Tyros backend server.
  • Dialer & Messaging Integration: Allows one-tap launching of your device's native phone dialer or WhatsApp to contact customers directly. Tyros does not read or intercept external call logs or private messages.
Note on Media Permissions: Tyros does not access, read, or upload your personal photos, videos, or external media files.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data or customer records to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. Data is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Dealership Administrators: Store owners and managers have administrative access to reports, enquiries, and staff performance for their respective stores.
  • Infrastructure & Service Providers: Encrypted hosting providers and crash-reporting services (Sentry) operating under strict confidentiality and security agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

5. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect all data handled by Tyros:

  • All network communication between the app and our server is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.2+.
  • Sensitive authentication tokens on your device are stored within secure hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Role-based access controls ensure that employees can only access data appropriate to their dealership assignment.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Customer follow-ups and operational records are retained for as long as active dealership accounts maintain their subscription with Tyros.

Dealership administrators or customers may request the deletion or correction of their data at any time by contacting our support team at the email address provided below. Upon verification, requested data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Tyros is a commercial business-to-business application designed solely for adults and business personnel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app functionality, legal requirements, or security best practices. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
